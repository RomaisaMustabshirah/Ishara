  • PinchRing + pinky folded. Thumb–index distance sets the level. Left = brightness, right = volume.
  • Open palmRest. Nothing changes, so you can move freely.
  • SwipeOpen palm, move fast left or right: previous / next track.
  • Fist (hold)Right = mute. Left = play / pause.

Ishara

Control music and light with your hands. Ishara means gesture in Urdu. Allow your camera, show your hands, and play.

Left pinchThumb–index distance sets the brightness
Right pinchThumb–index distance sets the volume
Open palmRest. Swipe left / right to change track

🔒 Everything runs on your device. Your camera feed is never uploaded or recorded.
Works best in Chrome or Edge on a laptop, in good light. The desktop version, which controls real system volume and brightness, is on GitHub.
